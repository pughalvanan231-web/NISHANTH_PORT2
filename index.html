ABOUT

I’m Joris, a motion director based in the Netherlands. Through Orchestrated I design and direct motion for brands and agencies. From motion systems to immersive motion and Rive animations. I care about motion that works: not an afterthought, but the backbone of digital storytelling.

IMPACTFUL COLLABORATIONS

I’ve had the chance to work with big and small brands across the globe, on everything from motion systems to one-pagers and immersive experiences. And picked up a few awards along the way.

WORKING TOGETHER

Orchestrated is built around one idea: motion should be a core part of the production process. It’s a powerful tool that can solve as many problems as it can create. Getting it right isn’t the animator’s job alone. Nor should it be a last-minute task for a developer: everyone involved should be Motion Minded™ from the start. And I help teams get there.

Other times I’m hands-on: directing motion inside your team, building motion mockups and Rive animations, or making tangible prototypes so you can try an idea before it’s built.
